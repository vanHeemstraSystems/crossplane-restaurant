# Editing

We will be using [Palmier](https://www.palmier.io), the video and audio editor wit AI built-in.

Import the audio track(s) recorded with QuickTime and Blackhole (see RECORDING](./RECORDING.md)).

Edit the audio by aligning SFX, Dialogue, and Music Scores. 

Split the composed audio track into individual scenes and save them as MP4 (audio) files.
